Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Welcome to Ritual (the “App”; formerly known as 300 Push Workout & Squats). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or phone number). Data you enter (e.g., exercise counts, streaks, preferences, and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

We may collect anonymized technical data for app reliability and purchase management, such as:

  • App version and device model
  • Basic usage data (e.g., which features are used)
  • Purchase events (managed via RevenueCat)
  • Push notification delivery data

Photos / Files: When you choose to export or save a progress card, the image is generated on-device and saved to your device’s Photos only with your action. We do not receive these files.

2. Notifications (Local & Push)

The App may send you notifications to remind you about daily workouts, streaks, milestones, or important updates. Notifications can be:

  • Local notifications — scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.

You can manage or disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

3. Purchases and Payments

The App offers optional paid access to Ritual Plus (entitlement: Premium). Current purchase options (pricing is shown in the App Store / paywall and varies by region):

  • Monthly subscription — auto-renewing monthly plan
  • Annual subscription — auto-renewing yearly plan
  • Lifetime (one-time) — a one-time purchase that unlocks Ritual Plus permanently; not a subscription

Ritual Plus unlocks:

  • Stats & records — trends, totals, and personal achievements beyond the free overview
  • Full ritual history — your complete calendar and history, not just the last 7 days
  • Progress cards — save weekly wins to Photos and share them

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID account settings. Lifetime is charged once and does not renew. We do not collect or store your payment information.

Purchase status is managed via RevenueCat, a third-party service that uses anonymous User IDs and may use device identifiers for fraud prevention and purchase validation purposes.

4. Data Security

We follow industry-standard practices to help protect data integrity and security. Because your workout data is stored locally and we do not collect personal data, privacy risks are minimized.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Anonymized purchase-related and usage events may be shared with RevenueCat strictly to operate and support in-app purchases.

6. Your Rights

You can manage or restore purchases at any time through your Apple ID account settings and the Restore option in the App.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

This App is not intended for children under 13 years old and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
